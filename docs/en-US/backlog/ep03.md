## Journey and validation criteria

| Story | Scenario and expected result |
| --- | --- |
| US-06 | Read observation values/units, interval, receipt and current status; absent/invalid is not zero. |
| US-07 | Apply an approved interval/period model and display kWh with coverage. |
| US-08 | Associate emissions with the factor snapshot used; missing factor preserves valid energy and marks CO₂e non-calculable. |
| US-09 | Trace a result to formula, parameters, versions, sources and limitations. |

Valid EP02 collection precedes calculation. Energy formula, coefficients, temporal boundaries, precision and coverage need decisions. [Methodology](../product/methodology) separates the emissions dimensional relationship from the pending energy model; prototype numbers are not coefficients.
