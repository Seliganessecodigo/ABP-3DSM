## Journey and validation criteria

| Story | Scenario and expected result |
| --- | --- |
| US-01 | Open the catalog, search/filter applications and navigate to detail with the same identity. No applications means empty; API/source failure means error; no filter matches does not erase the catalog. |

Source location follows its contract: city may be null; regional code, country, region and coordinates are required by the documented source. Incomplete historical records remain known without fabricated locations.

The catalog consumes EP02 status and EP03 evidence; querying it triggers neither collection nor calculation. The [user guide](../guide/monitoring) covers existing navigation.
