## Journey and validation criteria

NFRs apply to every story. Foundation tasks are finite: reproducible environments, persistence, integration, security, shared components and evidence. Completing a task does not retire its cross-cutting constraint.

Assessment combines functional criteria, failure tests, historical integrity, keyboard use and eight-language content. Planning, review and feedback (#134) are process evidence, separate from accessibility (RNF-07).

See [quality scenarios and gates](../product/quality) for expected evidence. Unapproved operational targets and policies remain [dependencies](../product/decisions).
